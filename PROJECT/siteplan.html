Website Site Plan: SafeChill NG

WDD 131 Final Project Proposal & Architectural Blueprint

1. Site Name & Reason for Selection

Site Name: SafeChill NG — Cold Chain Integrity & Perishable Food Safety Field Guide

Why Selected: Maintaining unbroken cold storage in warm climates is critical to preventing foodborne illness and reducing post-harvest food waste. This subject translates food science and cold chain temperature standards into a practical, everyday digital reference for food vendors, caterers, and households.

2. Site Purpose & Content Outline

Purpose: To provide clear, science-backed temperature guidelines, shelf-life limits, and power-outage emergency procedures for perishable foods across three cohesive pages:

  • Page 1 — Home (index.html): Introduces cold chain fundamentals, explains the Temperature Danger Zone (5°C–60°C), and includes an interactive JavaScript Temperature Status Checker.
  • Page 2 — Storage Directory (guide.html): A searchable and category-filterable directory of perishable items (Meat & Poultry, Dairy, Seafood, Fresh Produce) rendered dynamically from JavaScript objects, allowing users to bookmark items into a persistent "My Saved Storage List" via localStorage.
  • Page 3 — Cold Storage Self-Audit (audit.html): Features power-outage survival rules (the 2-hour/4-hour rule) and an accessible HTML form where vendors or homeowners can log fridge/freezer readings and receive instant safety feedback.
  • References Page (references.html): Linked in the footer to properly cite all external images and food safety standards (WHO, FAO, NAFDAC).

3. Target Audience & User Scenarios

Target Audience: Small-scale cold room operators, caterers, restaurant kitchen managers, market vendors, and home cooks in Nigeria seeking reliable temperature and food preservation standards.

  • Scenario 1: A caterer checks the exact safe refrigeration temperature and maximum storage days for raw poultry versus cooked stews before an event.
  • Scenario 2: A household experiences a 3-hour power outage and uses the self-audit guide to determine which items in their refrigerator remain safe to consume.
  • Scenario 3: A food vendor filters the storage directory for dairy and meat products and saves their daily checklist to localStorage for quick mobile reference.
