Three Core Temperature Zones

Bacterial growth accelerates rapidly when perishable foods linger outside verified cold or hot limits.

Safe Cold & Freezer

At or Below 4°C (Freezer ≤ -18°C)

Refrigeration at or below 4°C slows microbial multiplication in dairy, cooked dishes, and fresh cuts; -18°C or colder halts bacterial growth for long-term storage.

Danger Zone

5°C to 60°C

Pathogens can multiply rapidly in this window—especially in warm ambient climates. Limit exposure time and cool hot foods quickly into shallow containers.

Safe Hot Holding

Above 60°C

Keep hot soups, stews, and cooked proteins steaming above 60°C during service or transport until chilled properly for storage.
